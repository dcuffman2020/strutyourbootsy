# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Bootsy hits the runway in new faction-neutral transmog. The transparent crest replaces the previous logo in the addon and minimap launcher.
- No changes to contest rules, voting, or saved settings.

## 1.0.0

- Run one to five transmog contest rounds in Solo or Team mode, with a fresh rating ballot for each round.
- Combine each entry’s unrounded round averages with equal weight. Entries without a score in every round do not place in the final standings.
- Collect anonymous ratings with server-side eligibility checks. Spectators may rate any eligible entry; contestants cannot rate themselves or their own team, and the raid leader cannot vote.
- Reveal awards after the final round. The leader confirms tied podium placements after recording in-game rolls, then reveals the winners.
- Preserve the in-game outfit review, minimap launcher, and solo practice options from the beta.

## 0.7.0-beta.1

- Team contests, leadership nominations, team voting, and the in-contest outfit gallery. See [beta notes](docs/BETA_0_7_0.md).
